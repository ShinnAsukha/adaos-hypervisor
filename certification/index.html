FAQ

How long is the certification valid?

Three years from issue date. Recert requires either a 25-question delta exam ($49) or proof of contributing to the OXware ecosystem (PR merged, plugin published, talk delivered).

Is there a free practice exam?

Yes. 15-question diagnostic at docs/#oca-practice after launch. No registration required.

What if I fail?

Retake at $49 within 90 days. We share the per-domain score so you know what to study.

Can my employer pay?

Yes. Invoice + W-9 / VAT receipt available. Partner-tier employers get bulk discounts (5 seats = 20% off, 25+ = 35% off).

Is there an advanced cert?

OXware Certified Architect (OCAr) launches Q4 2026. Hands-on cluster scenario lab. $299. OCA is a prerequisite.

Languages?

v1 launches in English. Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Chinese translations land within 60 days of launch.
