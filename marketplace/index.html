Build & Share Your Own Plugins

Anyone can develop plugins directly inside the OXware panel and run them on their own system. The marketplace catalog above is curated — only the maintainer adds entries to keep it safe and trusted.

  • 1 Open Settings > Plugins > Develop in your panel. Scaffold a template, write your code, and validate it in the browser — no external tools needed.
  • 2 Upload and enable the plugin on your own OXware install. It runs locally on your system — you stay in full control.
  • 3 Want to share it with the community? Publish your plugin in GitHub Discussions. Useful, well-tested plugins may be picked up and added to this curated catalog.
