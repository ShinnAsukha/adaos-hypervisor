Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 9, 2026

GDPR Compliant

Short version: OXware is self-hosted software. When you run OXware on your own servers, all your VM data, credentials, and usage information stays on your infrastructure — we never see it. This policy covers only what happens when you visit oxware.top and when you purchase a commercial license.

1. Who We Are

OXware is an open-source hypervisor management panel developed and maintained by the OXware team. Contact: root@oxware.top

2. Data We Collect

On oxware.top (this website):

  • Standard web server access logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) — retained for 30 days
  • Contact form submissions (name, email, message) — used only to respond to inquiries
  • License purchase information (name, email) — processed via our payment provider

OXware panel (self-hosted software):

  • All data (VMs, users, credentials, audit logs) is stored entirely on your own server
  • OXware does not transmit any operational data to our servers
  • Optional telemetry (anonymous install count) can be disabled in config — no PII is collected

3. How We Use Data

  • To respond to support and sales inquiries
  • To deliver license keys and activation emails
  • To improve the website and documentation
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing

4. Cookies

oxware.top uses only essential cookies necessary for site functionality (session management, CSRF protection). We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics.

5. Third-Party Services

  • GitHub — source code hosting, issue tracking. Governed by GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Cloudflare — CDN for font-awesome assets. Governed by Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • WhatsApp Business — sales/support channel. Messages governed by Meta privacy policy

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing of your personal data

To exercise any of these rights, email root@oxware.top. We will respond within 30 days.

7. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, or destruction. The OXware panel itself features JWT authentication, RBAC, 2FA, account lockout, IP allowlisting, audit logging, and encrypted credential storage.

To report a security vulnerability, see our Security Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

OXware is intended for professional and commercial use. We do not knowingly collect personal data from individuals under 16 years of age.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced via the GitHub repository. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

10. Contact
